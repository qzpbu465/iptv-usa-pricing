## One important point about IPTV in the USA

IPTV is simply a method of delivering television over an internet connection. The legal question is different: a provider needs the appropriate rights to retransmit copyrighted programming. The U.S. Copyright Office explains that internet retransmission of copyrighted television programming generally requires authorization from the copyright owners, and the Department of Justice has continued to take action against services that stream copyrighted programming without authorization.

For that reason, when comparing any IPTV USA service, it is worth looking beyond channel counts and asking what content the provider is authorized to distribute.
